## 🎯 What to Look For

### Good Images ✅
- **Clear focus** on flowers
- **Bright colors** (vibrant, not dull)
- **Good lighting** (natural or studio)
- **Clean background** (white, blurred, or simple)
- **High resolution** (at least 800px width)

### Avoid ❌
- Blurry or out-of-focus
- Dark or poorly lit
- Busy/distracting backgrounds
- Watermarks
- Very small images (under 500px)
