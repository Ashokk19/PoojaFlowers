# Puja Flowers Images

## 📸 Add Your Flower Images Here

Place your puja flower images in this folder with these exact names:

### Required Images:

1. **marigold.jpg** - Marigold (Genda) flowers
2. **jasmine.jpg** - Jasmine (Chameli) flowers  
3. **lotus.jpg** - Lotus (Kamal) flowers
4. **hibiscus.jpg** - Hibiscus (Gudhal) flowers
5. **rose.jpg** - Rose (Gulab) flowers

### Image Guidelines:

- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Size**: Recommended 800x800px (square format)
- **File Size**: Keep under 500KB for fast loading
- **Quality**: High quality, well-lit, clear focus on flowers
- **Background**: Clean, preferably white or light background

### Where to Get Images:

1. **Free Stock Photos**:
   - Unsplash: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/marigold-flower
   - Pexels: https://www.pexels.com/search/puja-flowers/
   - Pixabay: https://pixabay.com/images/search/marigold/

2. **AI Generated** (if needed):
   - DALL-E, Midjourney, or Stable Diffusion
   - Prompt: "Fresh [flower name] flowers for hindu puja, high quality product photography, white background"

3. **Your Own Photos**:
   - Take photos of fresh flowers
   - Use good lighting
   - Square crop for best results

### Quick Download Links:

**Marigold**: 
- Search: "marigold flowers puja" on Unsplash/Pexels
- Look for: Orange/yellow marigold garlands or loose flowers

**Jasmine**:
- Search: "jasmine flowers white" 
- Look for: White jasmine strings or loose flowers

**Lotus**:
- Search: "pink lotus flower"
- Look for: Pink or white lotus in full bloom

**Hibiscus**:
- Search: "red hibiscus flower"
- Look for: Bright red hibiscus, single flower

**Rose**:
- Search: "red rose flowers"
- Look for: Fresh red roses, preferably in bunch
