# 🌸 Pooja Flower Images

## Add Your Flower Images Here

Place these 5 flower images in this directory:

1. **marigold.jpg** - Orange/yellow marigold flowers
2. **jasmine.jpg** - White jasmine flowers  
3. **lotus.jpg** - Pink or white lotus flowers
4. **hibiscus.jpg** - Red hibiscus flowers
5. **rose.jpg** - Red rose flowers

## Image Requirements:
- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Size**: 800x800px (square format recommended)
- **File Size**: Under 500KB each
- **Quality**: High resolution, well-lit, clean background

## Quick Download:
Visit **Unsplash** or **Pexels** and search for:
- "marigold flowers puja"
- "jasmine flowers white" 
- "pink lotus flower"
- "red hibiscus flower"
- "red rose flowers"

## After Adding Images:
Your slideshow will automatically display these real-world flower images instead of the emoji fallbacks!

See `POOJA_FLOWER_IMAGES_GUIDE.md` in the project root for detailed instructions.
