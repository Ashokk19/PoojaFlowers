# 🌸 Add Your Hero Background Image

## ✅ What I've Done

Changed the homepage to display a **full-width background image** with puja flowers (like your screenshot), with text overlay on the left side.

## 📸 Add Your Image (2 Steps)

### Step 1: Get a Beautiful Flower Image

**Download from:**

1. **Pexels** (Free, no attribution): https://www.pexels.com
   - Search: "puja flowers bouquet"
   - Search: "marigold jasmine lotus mix"
   - Search: "sunflower rose lily arrangement"

2. **Unsplash** (Free): https://unsplash.com
   - Search: "fresh flower arrangement"
   - Search: "colorful flowers bouquet"

3. **Or use the image like your screenshot:**
   - Search: "sunflower rose lily mixed bouquet"
   - Look for: Vibrant mix of yellow sunflowers, red roses, white lilies

**Image Requirements:**
- **Size**: At least 1920x1080 pixels (Full HD)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Orientation**: Landscape (horizontal)
- **Style**: Colorful, fresh, vibrant flowers

### Step 2: Add to Your Project

1. **Save** your downloaded image as: `hero-flowers.jpg`

2. **Place** the image in:
   ```
   C:\Flora\public\images\hero-flowers.jpg
   ```
   
   ⚠️ **Important**: The file must be named exactly `hero-flowers.jpg` (lowercase, with hyphen)

4. **Refresh** browser: `Ctrl + F5`

✅ **Done!** Your hero section will show the beautiful background image!

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Image should be wide** (landscape orientation)
2. **Bright, colorful flowers** work best
3. **Right side** should have good flower details (text covers left side)
4. **High resolution** for crisp display
5. **Compress** to under 1MB for fast loading

---

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**Image not showing?**

1. Check filename is exactly: `hero-flowers.jpg`
2. Check location: `C:\Flora\public\images\hero-flowers.jpg`
3. Clear browser cache: `Ctrl + Shift + R`
4. Check file extension (not hero-flowers.jpg.jpg)

**Text hard to read?**

The overlay automatically adjusts - white gradient from left makes text readable.
